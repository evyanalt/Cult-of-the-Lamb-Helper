The Crowns

Information about the crowns, what they mean and their wearers.
Disclaimer: Not all data is confirmed. If not, it is marked.

Green Crown — Leshy

Shape: Green diamond

Motif: See no evil

"Through the dirt I would wade until one day... I found a Crown... or perhaps it found me?" — Leshy

Every chaos has an order (Not official)

Leshy is the last known wearer of the Green Crown. He reigns over Darkwood and is the bishop of chaos. The crown reflects the uncontrolled abundance of nature—growth, decay, and wild unpredictability.

Leshy claims to have found the crown while burrowing through the dirt as a mortal creature. However, he questions whether he found the crown, or if the crown found him—hinting that the crowns may possess some form of will or sentience.

Leshy follows the motif "See no evil", because Narinder gouged out his eyes during their conflict. He now wears thick, bloodstained bandages. Despite this, his emotions are still visible through the shapes formed in the wrappings.

Upon defeating Leshy, the player earns the achievement "See No Evil". Later in the game, he can be recruited as a follower and will eventually ask to return to Darkwood, where he grants his relic: The Eye of Leshy.

Yellow crown - Heket

Shape: Inverted yellow triangle

Motif: Speak no evil

"Your sins are many... They shall starve!" — Heket

Every hunger has an abundance that fills it (Not official)

Heket is the first and last known wearer of the Yellow Crown. She reigns over Anura and is the bishop of famine. The crown reflects decay, scarcity, and corruption. Yellow symbolizes both rot and toxicity in her domain.

Despite having her throat slit by Narinder, Heket is still able to speak. This suggests the crown compensates for her injury. When she loses the crown, her speech becomes slower and more fragmented.

Unlike the Red Crown, the Yellow Crown does not appear to transform into weapons, but it still grants immense power, allowing Heket to ascend to godhood.

"The Bishops… my family... Have I not suffered enough?" — Heket after her defeat

Blue crown - Kallamar

Shape: Blue circle

Motif: Hear no evil

"I do not want to die!" — Kallamar

Every sickness has its cure (Not official)

Kallamar is the bishop of pestilence and rules over Anchordeep. The Blue Crown reflects disease, decay, and the damp environments where illness thrives.

Kallamar is notably cowardly and paranoid, constantly fearing death. Despite wielding divine power, his fear overshadows his authority.

His ears were torn off by Narinder, giving him the motif "Hear no evil". This is reflected in the achievement earned upon his defeat.

After recruitment, he expresses regret and fear of his siblings. His quest leads to retrieving his relic: Kallamar’s Ear.

Purple crown - Shamura

Shape: Purple crescent

Motif: Think no evil

"I was the eldest... I should have known better." — Shamura

Every war has its end (Not official)

Shamura is the eldest bishop and represents war and strategy. They reign over the Silk Cradle and possess deep knowledge of fate and planning.

Narinder split their skull open, leaving their brain exposed and their memory fragmented. As a result, Shamura speaks in broken thoughts and incomplete sentences.

The Purple Crown appears to stabilize their mind, allowing them to function despite their injury. Without it, their intelligence would likely collapse entirely.

Unlike the others, Shamura lost not a sense, but their very mind—making them the most tragic of the bishops.

Red crown - The Lamb/Narinder

Shape: Eye / Bell

Motif: Do no evil (Not official)

"Take this, my Crown..." — Narinder

Every life has a debt (Not official)

The Red Crown is the most powerful and unique of all crowns. Unlike the others, it is clearly sentient, reacting and blinking.

It can shapeshift into weapons and tools, granting the Lamb immense combat abilities. It also grants resurrection, binding the Lamb to Narinder’s will.

The crown grows stronger by consuming Hearts of Heretics, allowing the Lamb to reshape reality within their cult.

It represents action and consequence, completing the set of Bishop motifs.

Cyan crown - Yngya

Shape: Cyan crystal

Motif: Feel no evil (Not official)

"The frost will take what is owed..." — Yngya

Every winter has its spring (Not official)

Yngya is the god of winter and ruler of Woolhaven. Her crown represents preservation, stillness, and eventual renewal.

She avoided the purge by freezing time within her domain. Her crown grants control over ice and storms.

She follows "Feel no evil", having sacrificed emotion and warmth to endure eternal winter.

Her frozen form reflects both protection and isolation.
