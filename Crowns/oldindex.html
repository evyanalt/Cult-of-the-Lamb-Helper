The Crowns

Information about the crows, what they mean and the wearers.
Disclaimer: Not all of the data is confirmed. If not, it will be mentioned.

The green crown

Last worn by: Leshy

Shape: Green diamond

Motif: See no evil

"Through the dirt I would wade until one day... I found a Crown... or perhaps it found me?" — Leshy, to the Lamb regarding how he found the Green Crown

Every chaos has an order (Not official)

Leshy

Leshy is the last known wearer of the green crown. He reigns over the Darkwood and is the bishop of chaos. The Green Crown ties in with the motifs surrounding Leshy as it is reminiscent of the abundance of nature found within his realm.

Leshy claims to have found the Green Crown will burrowing through the dirt when he was merely a mortal "burrower". He then ponders whether it was he who found the crown or whether the crown found him. It still is unclear if the crowns are sentient or not

Leshy has the motif "See no evil", Because The One Who Waits (Or narinder) gouged it out, thus the thick bloodstained bandages around Leshy's head. His emotions can still be seen from the shape on the bandages.

When leshy is defeated for the first time you'll be granted the achievement "See no evil" because, as already said leshy has no eye anymore.

Later in the game, when defeating the bishops for a second time you will be able to get Leshy as a follower. Then after a bit they will give you a quest to get some flowers from the darkwood. Then a little bit later he will ask you to convert him into a demon and take him back to his temple in darkood, Where he then will grant you his relic, The eye of leshy.

The yellow crown

Last worn by: Heket

Shape: Inverted yellow triangle

Motif: Speak no evil

"Your sins are many, and for that your loyal Followers must SUFFER! They shall starve!" — Heket, the first and last known wearer of the Yellow Crown

Evey hunger has an abundance that fills it (Not official)

Heket

Heket is the first and last known wearer of the yellow crown. She reigns over Anura and is the bishop of Famine. The Yellow Crown ties in with the motifs surrounding Heket as yellow could symbolize the withering and decay of plant life, representing the effects of famine and scarcity in the Anura. Yellow can also evoke associations with toxicity and corruption. The abundance of spores and mushrooms in the realm might be accompanied by a sense of danger and contamination. Yellow hues can signify poisonous or harmful substances present in the environment, representing the Heket and the Yellow Crown's influence over the land and its inhabitants.

As with the other crowns it is unclear whether the Yellow Crown is sentient or not. Although it is clear that the Yellow Crown is an immense source of power and the reason Heket was able to ascend to godhood.

The Yellow Crown, much like the Green Crown, Blue Crown and Purple Crown but unlike the Red Crown, is not seen transforming into weapons or tools in order to aid Heket in any way. The Yellow Crown does seem to help Heket compensate for her slit throat, however, as she is still able to speak despite her injury. Supporting this theory is the fact that when she loses her crown, and is converted into a Follower, she is shown to struggle more with speaking. Without her crown she says fewer words and takes longer pauses between statements.

The Bishops… my family. Have they not suffered enough? Have I not suffered enough? We fought, pathetic vessel. We bled. We grieved. And yet the Red Crown wants more. No more.— Heket expressing her anguish to the Lamb once she learns the plan of the Old Faith had failed.

Heket has the motif "Speak no evil", Because her throat was slit out by The One Who Waits (narinder).

The blue crown

Last worn by: Kallamar

Shape: Blue circle

Motif: Hear no evil

"I do not want to die! I have seen what lies beyond, and I will not go!" — Kallamar, pleading for his life before his battle with the Lamb

Every sickness has its cure (Not official)

Kallamar

Kallamar is the last known wearer of the blue crown. He reigns over Anchordeep and is the bishop of Pestilence. The Blue Crown reflects Kallamar’s domain of the deep sea and the spreading of illness; its cool blue color often represents the cold, damp environments where disease and rot thrive among the docks and waves of his realm.

Kallamar is characterized by his intense cowardice and paranoia. Unlike his siblings, who meet the Lamb with varying degrees of stoicism or rage, Kallamar is visibly terrified of death. It is suggested that the Blue Crown grants him the power to summon undead and manipulate the water, yet his fear often overshadows his divine authority.

Kallamar has the motif "Hear no evil" because his ears were torn off by Narinder during their ancient conflict. This is why his head is heavily bandaged on the sides. This motif is further cemented by the achievement "Hear No Evil," earned upon his defeat. His loss of hearing symbolizes his refusal to listen to the warnings of the coming change, or perhaps a forced silence to the whispers of the Void.

In the post-game, after being recruited as a follower, Kallamar expresses deep regret and fear of his siblings' shadows. His questline involves retrieving Crystal Shards from Anchordeep. Eventually, he will ask to be taken back to his temple as a demon to retrieve his relic, Kallamar's Ear, allowing him to finally "hear" the world again through the Lamb’s power.

The purple crown

Last worn by: Shamura

Shape: Purple moon

Motif: Think no evil

"Four Bishops of the Old Faith. Five, there were once. I was the eldest. I should have known better." — Shamura, reflecting on the betrayal of Narinder

Every war has its end (Not official)

Shamura

Shamura is the last known wearer of the purple crown. They reign over the Silk Cradle and are the bishop of War. As the eldest and once wisest of the Bishops, Shamura’s Purple Crown represents their sophisticated understanding of strategy and the complex web of fate they weave from their spider-infested domain.

The Purple Crown is unique in how it interacts with Shamura's shattered mind. Because Narinder split Shamura’s skull open, their memory is fragmented. Shamura often speaks in rhymes or pauses mid-sentence, struggling to hold onto the present. The crown seems to be the only thing keeping their vast, tactical mind functioning despite the physical trauma to their brain.

Shamura has the motif "Think no evil" because of the horrific head wound inflicted by Narinder, which left their brain exposed and bandaged. This corresponds to the achievement "Think No Evil." While the other Bishops lost sensory organs, Shamura lost their very mind—the seat of thought—making them a tragic figure who remembers the love they once had for Narinder even as they plot his destruction.

Once Shamura joins your cult, they provide deep lore about the history of the Bishops. Their quest involves gathering Silk from their old realm. Like the others, they will eventually request a journey back to the Silk Cradle as a demon to recover their relic, Shamura's Skull, a grim reminder of the price they paid for the Old Faith.

The red crown

Last worn by: The Lamb

Shape: Red eye / Bell

Motif: Do no evil (Not official)

"Take this, my Crown. In return, all I ask is that you start a Cult in my name." — Narinder, bestowing the Red Crown upon the Lamb

Every life has a debt (Not official)

The Lamb

The Red Crown is the most versatile of the artifacts, known for its ability to shapeshift into various weapons and tools. Originally belonging to Narinder (The One Who Waits), it was passed to the Lamb to act as his vessel. Unlike the other crowns, it is overtly sentient, blinking and reacting to the Lamb's actions.

The Red Crown completes the set of the Bishops' motifs with "Do no evil". While the others lost their physical senses, the Red Crown represents the weight of action and the burden of leadership. It is the only crown shown to consume the "Hearts of Heretics" to evolve, allowing the wearer to rewrite the very laws of reality within their cult.

Throughout the Lamb's journey, the crown serves as both a weapon and a leash. It grants the power of resurrection, ensuring the Lamb can never truly die so long as the cult survives. However, it also binds the Lamb to Narinder's will—at least until the student is ready to surpass the master.

The cyan crown

Last worn by: Yngya

Shape: Cyan diamond

Motif: Feel no evil (Not official)

"The frost will take what is owed, but from the slush, a new spring shall rise." — Yngya, reflecting on the cycle of the seasons

Every winter has its spring (Not official)

Yngya

Yngya is the bearer of the Cyan Crown and the God of Winter. She reigns over the frozen peak of Woolhaven. The Cyan Crown is unique among the artifacts as it represents the natural cycle of the seasons—specifically the numbing preservation of winter and the eventual renewal of spring.

In the lore of the Woolhaven expansion, Yngya is often seen as a "forgotten" sibling or a separate deity who avoided the Great Purge by freezing time within her realm. Her crown grants her total control over ice and storms, which she uses to protect her flock from the influence of the other Bishops.

Yngya follows the motif "Feel no evil". This is represented by her numbed, frozen state; she is emotionally distant and physically cold to the touch, having sacrificed her ability to "feel" in order to endure the eternal winter. Her hands are often depicted as frozen or crystalline, symbolizing the price she paid to keep her realm isolated from the chaos of the Old Faith.
